# Data Jobs Dashboard with Power BI

![Dashboard Page 1](/Images/Dashboard_page_1.png)

## Introduction

This project analyzes **data job postings** to better understand trends in *job demand*, *salary expectations*, *required skills*, *job platforms*, and *workplace benefits*. The dashboard was built in **Power BI** using a real-world dataset of data-related job postings, including roles such as **Data Analyst**, **Data Scientist**, **Data Engineer**, **Business Analyst**, and **Machine Learning Engineer**.

The goal of this project is to create an **_interactive dashboard_** that helps job seekers, career changers, and job market researchers explore the data job market in a clear and organized way. Users can compare job titles, review salary trends, analyze job counts over time, and drill through into specific roles to better understand what each position offers.

This project also gave me hands-on experience with the full **_business intelligence workflow_**, including data cleaning, data transformation, DAX measures, dashboard design, and insight generation.

## Dashboard Overview

### Page 1: High-Level Market View

![Dashboard Page 1](/Images/Dashboard_page_1.png)

This page provides a broad overview of the data job market. It highlights key metrics such as total job postings, median salaries, job trends over time, and the most common data-related job titles. The goal of this page is to give users a quick snapshot of the market and help them identify which roles are most in demand.


### Page 2: Job Title Drill Through

![Dashboard Page 2](/Images/Dashboard_page_2.png)

This page gives users a deeper look into a selected job title. After choosing a role from the main dashboard, users can drill through to view more detailed information such as salary ranges, work-from-home availability, degree requirements, health insurance mentions, job platforms, job types, and global job locations. This allows users to compare roles more closely and better understand what each career path may offer.

## Conclusion

By combining **Power Query**, **DAX measures**, and visual storytelling, the dashboard makes it easier to compare job titles, understand salary patterns, identify high-demand roles, and analyze job benefits.

Through this project, I strengthened my skills in **data cleaning**, **data modeling**, **dashboard design**, and **business intelligence reporting**.
